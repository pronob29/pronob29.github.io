Course Support
======
Assisted in teaching **STA 296 (Statistical Methods and Motivations)** and **STA 210 (Introduction to Statistical Reasoning)**, where I helped undergraduate students understand foundational statistical concepts and data analysis techniques. I provided support with course materials, answered questions, and reinforced statistical reasoning to improve student engagement and comprehension.

Office Hours and Mentorship
======
Held regular office hours to provide one-on-one guidance, addressing individual student needs and challenges with assignments, statistical software tools, and exam preparation. I created a supportive environment for students to ask questions and build confidence in their statistical skills.

Machine Learning Integration
======
Incorporated machine learning concepts into the curriculum, giving students an introduction to **advanced statistical methods** and their applications in real-world data scenarios. This practical approach to teaching enriched their understanding of the field and its industry applications.
