Course Development and Support
======
Assisted in the development and delivery of **IS 603 (Decision Making Support Systems)**, **IS 733 (Data Mining)**, and **IS 734 (Data Analytics for Cybersecurity)**. Focused on providing students with hands-on experience using **AI and data science tools**. I helped design course materials and ensured students had practical skills that aligned with current industry standards.

Practical Application
======
Integrated real-world case studies and projects to give students practical exposure to **AI and cybersecurity analytics**. My role included guiding students through complex problem-solving scenarios and supporting their development of skills in **data mining, decision support systems,** and **cybersecurity**.
